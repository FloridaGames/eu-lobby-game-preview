EU Lobby Game: Future Burger Act (2026)

Onboarding

How EU legislation actually gets made

You are a professional lobbyist working on the Future Burger Act (2026). Over three phases you will try to move five contested articles towards your client's preferred position — with a limited budget, limited actions per round, and a reputation to protect.

Phase 1

European Commission

The Commission holds the right of initiative: it writes the first draft. Influence here shapes the legislative content everyone else will argue over.

Phase 2

European Parliament

720 directly elected MEPs, organised in political groups, table amendments and vote a Parliament position. You need 361 seats behind you.

Phase 3

Council of the EU

27 national governments negotiate. A qualified majority means 55% of member states representing 65% of the EU population.

Resources & scoring

Budget (€50,000 to start), Credibility and Public Support are tracked throughout. Each phase runs 2 rounds with a maximum of 2 actions per round, and every action needs a written justification.
